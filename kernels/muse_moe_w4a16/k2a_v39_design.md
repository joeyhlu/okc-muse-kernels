## 1. What J1 measured (a2_lab, v3.3 NC1 = vid 0, layer routing, 2 routings × 20 rounds; `box_out/a2b_lab.log`)

The probes reuse v3.3's own `fmode` switches:
- `f6` stores split parts like whole ones: no reds, no epilogue. This is the ceiling.
- `f7` keeps the consumers' FP32 reds into h/y but drops the epilogue finalize: no fence, counter, readback,
  SwiGLU/Σz² or re-zeroing.

Readings:
1. **f7 ≈ f6 at every bs.** The consumer reds are free; the whole split cost is the epilogue chain.
   - The chain runs on the epilogue warp: bar, then `__threadfence`, then the counter atomic, then for the last
     contributor a fence, h/y readback, finalize and re-zeroing.
   - Each of those round trips queues behind the SM's in-flight bulk data, ~2–3 µs.
   - What must go is the cross-CTA *handshake*, not the partial-sum traffic.
2. The cost is split between the two kernels:
   - bs1: ~2.3 µs in K1, ~0.6 in K2;
   - bs16/32: ~1.7 in each;
   - the traced tail falls 6.8–7.2 → 3.5–3.8 µs at bs16/32.
3. **v3.3 NC1 is a better base than the shipped v3.6 role split at bs16/32 once splits are free.**
   - vid 0's f6 is 59.6 (bs16) / 96.4 (bs32) µs. The shipped plan in the v3.8 bench is 64.6 / 98.9 µs, run on the same
     layer routings and seeds.
   - So a split-free vid 0 beats ship by up to −5.0 / −2.5 µs.
   - v3.9 therefore builds on vid 0 at every bucket.
4. The v3.8 package (B1–B4) is dead (`box_out/k38_run.log`):
   - L2 prefetch: +0.3…+4.7 µs;
   - tilt / B3 / B4: −0.2…−1.4 µs, every row KILL by the §5 gates.

## 2. Mechanism (vids 6 / 7 = vid 0 in 2- / 4-CTA clusters)

- **Launch.** K1 and K2 are launched with `cudaLaunchAttributeClusterDimension` (CL, 1, 1); K2 keeps PDL and K3 is
  unchanged. Ranges are vid 0's stream-K ranges, B(c) = ⌊S c / G⌋ (no tilt).
- **Which items go through DSMEM.** A split item uses DSMEM when every CTA touching it, clo = `cta_of(a0)` through
  chi = `cta_of(a0 + 15)`, is in the same cluster (`clo / CL == chi / CL`).
  - The owner is chi, the CTA holding the item's last stage. It owns at most one item: the one holding its first stage.
  - Contributor clo + k writes into slot k.
- **Contributor.** Its part is processed first (tail-first order). It sends its unscaled NC1 accumulators with
  `st.async.shared::cluster.mbarrier::complete_tx::bytes.v4.f32` into the owner's slot, 16 B per lane, 4 KB per
  contributor. Then it skips the flush and the epilogue hand-off.
- **Owner.** Its part is processed second.
  - It waits on its receive mbarrier with `mbarrier.try_wait.parity.acquire.cluster`; the wait is bounded and sets
    the error flag on timeout.
  - It forms gs·own + gs·p₀ (+ gs·p₁ …) with `__fmul_rn`/`__fadd_rn`, in slot order. For a 2-way split that is
    exactly the value of today's two FP32 reds onto zero, in either order.
  - It flushes the item as a whole one. In K1 that is SwiGLU → bf16 → interS, the same function and rounding as the
    epilogue's finalize. In K2 it is the y store plus the whole-item Σz² reds into ss (whole items already do this).
- **Arming.** `cl_setup`: thread 0 initialises the receive barrier and arms `expect_tx` with (chi − clo) × 4 KB
  (0 when the CTA owns nothing), then a cluster barrier. No st.async can land before its barrier is armed.
- **Items spanning clusters** keep v3.3's global protocol unchanged. The epilogue walk skips DSMEM items with the same
  predicate, so the named-barrier ids stay in step.
- **Numerics.**
  - 2-way K1 splits: bit-identical to vid 0.
  - K2 DSMEM items: their Σz² is summed in the whole-item order instead of the epilogue's. Same ulp class; ss is
    accumulated by FP32 reds today as well.
  - 3+-way splits (bs1): the reduction order becomes fixed (owner + slots in order), so they are more deterministic
    than today's atomics.
- **Smem.** vid 0's 68.0 KB + 16 B + (CL − 1) × 4 KB: 72 KB / 80 KB per CTA at 2 CTAs/SM. Registers are vid 0's plus
  a few.

Coverage, emulated on the 28 A2 routings (`scratchpad/v38/emu39.py`):
- Every owner's armed bytes equal the bytes sent to it; contributor slots are unique; 0 failures.
- At CL = 4, 73–75% of split parts at bs8–32 go through DSMEM, and every CTA has ≤ 1 global split part (today 2).
- At bs1 the share is 62% in K1 and 26% in K2.

Model: the f7 ceiling × the DSMEM share, plus the relief from an epilogue warp that is no longer saturated
(≤ 1 chain per CTA, processed first). Raw: bs8 −1.2, bs16 −2.7, bs32 −2.6 µs against vid 0, and bs16/32 a further
−1…−5 against ship. ×0.5 (R7): bs16 ≈ −1.4, bs32 ≈ −1.3 against vid 0.

Gate (coordinator): ≤ −2 µs against ship at bs16 AND bs32, no bucket slower by > 0.3 µs. Kill: mean over bs8–32
> −1 µs.
