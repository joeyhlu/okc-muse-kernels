# K2A v3.2 (experiment): wgmma hot-expert path, NOT shipped (2026-10-24)

File: `k2a_v32.py` (`# nonce k2a-v32-1`) = v3.1 + a hot chain for bs 128/256: experts with > 32 tokens go to
P0 (pack their x rows into wgmma's core-matrix layout) → H1 (W13, wgmma m64n64k16, RS form: A = v3.1's dequantized
fragments, B = the stage's X slice via a no-swizzle K-major descriptor, same 18 KB bulk weight stages) → K1 cold →
K2 cold → H2 (W2; H1 writes the intermediate straight into the B layout) → K3. Every launch after P0 is PDL.
Hot/cold split by a per-kernel `hot_sel` in the routing (cold kernels skip hot experts, hot kernels skip cold).

Correct everywhere (rel-L2 3.74–3.77e-3, 9 cases incl. 1/8 hot experts × 64/128 tokens, bs40/128 skewed, 257, 320);
SASS not serialized (16 HGMMA, one `DEPBAR gsb0,0x0` per item); eager no-caching memcheck 0 errors
(`box_out/k32_memcheck_eager.log`).

## Why it loses on the judge's routings (`box_out/k32_lab3.log`)

| input | hot experts (> 32 tokens) | v3.1 | v3.2 chain | cold kernels only | P0 + H1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bs128 seed 102 | none | 235.6 | 246.9 | 235.8 | 12.8 |
| bs128 seed 103 | 38, 34 | 242.3 | 262.5 | 187.2 | 53.2 |
| bs256 seed 102 | 53, 33 | 319.6 | 358.0 | 259.8 | 66.7 |
| bs256 seed 103 | 77, 75 | 327.7 | 368.1 | 238.2 | 79.3 |

- The judge's "normal" routings have only **0–2 experts above 32 tokens**, with 33–77 tokens. The hot kernels then
  hold ~2 experts' work (128–256 items for 264 CTAs): ramp-dominated, plus three extra launch boundaries
  (+11 µs even with no hot expert at all).
- N = 64 pass granularity wastes work: 75–77 tokens = 2 full passes.
- But those 1–2 warm experts cost v3.1 **55–90 µs** (cold-only drops by that much): they are the bs128/256 lever.

## What would make it pay (not built; estimates)
1. Hot and cold items in the **same K1/K2 launches** (per-item path; unified 50 KB stage, NST 2, ≤ 102 regs at
   2 CTAs/SM — H1 alone is 96 regs + 48 B spill). Removes H1/H2 boundaries and ramp; P0 stays (PDL, weights first).
2. **Per-pass N** from {16, 32, 48, 64} (one wgmma instantiation each) and a lower threshold (> 16 tokens), so a
   75-token expert is 64 + 16, not 2 × 64.
3. Estimated: bs256 −30 to −50 µs, bs128 −10 to −30 µs (seed-dependent; one judge-like seed has no hot expert).
