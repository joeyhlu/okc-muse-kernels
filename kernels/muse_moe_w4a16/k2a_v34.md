No variant gains; the ship gate (≥ +4% overall or ≥ +8% at bs32) is not reachable with these, so no judge run.

- **Fused K12 degenerates into a grid barrier.** Under stream-K each expert's W13 items are spread across whole CTA
  ranges, so an expert's W13 completes only near the end of the W13 phase; the W2 phase then waits as K2 did, plus
  the per-item hand-off fences. Making it pay needs an expert-major interleaved schedule (every CTA works expert e's
  W13 slice, then e+1's, with W2(e) lagging), which turns most items into split items.
- **Warm path at bs16/32**: the HOTCAP machinery (P0 launch, 96-reg spilling kernel, 2-stage ring) costs ~3.5% even
  with no warm expert; the 9–32-token experts there don't win enough back.

## Where the small-T time goes (`box_out/k34_lab2.log`, v3.3 plan, seed 101)

| bs | K1 | +K2 | +K3 (full) | K3 alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 28.4 | 42.0 | 44.1 | 2.1 |
| 8 | 53.0 | 77.9 | 79.8 | 1.9 |
| 16 | 71.7 | 105.6 | 108.0 | 2.4 |
| 32 | 92.6 | 134.7 | 136.7 | 2.0 |

- K3 is now ~2 µs (folding it into K2 would save ≤ ~1–1.5%).
- K2 runs at the same rate as K1 (≈ 3.0–3.1 TB/s including its boundary): the K1→K2 boundary is no longer a big item.
- **The ~10% math tax is the SM clock**: inside K1 the SM clock is 1980 MHz with the consumer math compiled out and
  1786–1806 MHz with decode (+ mma) on (−9.3%, power cap), at every bs. Decode alone draws the same as decode + mma.
  The stream rate tracks the SM clock (math-off steady ~4.0 TB/s → ~3.6 with math), i.e. per-SM ingest scales with
  the clock. The v3.1 integer-op cut (−25% INT per word) did not move it. Levers left there are power, not issue.
- bs32 is at its rate bound: 443 MB at ~3.3 TB/s effective ≈ 134 µs; the 9% re-pass excess (NC1) costs about what
  NC2's second mma chunk costs.
