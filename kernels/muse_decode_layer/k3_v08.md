Rules kept:
- **PDL.** None of the new kernels uses PDL. K2A v3 launches its K1 without the PDL attribute, and its K2/K3 are
  PDL-chained internally, so the chain is consistent.
- **Graph capture.** No host syncs in any hot function (asserted). There are no raw pointers or descriptors in
  prepare state: tensors only, pointers taken at call time.
- **Size:** file 244 KB.
- **No `__trap()`.** The tail has no waits. The GEMV fixup counters self-reset, and the R1B self-test checks a repeat
  call. K2A v3's bounded waits are as reviewed in the MoE file.

**prepare (cold compile, box):** K1 43.5 s at import; K2A v3 35.4 s; R1B 40.7 s; tail about 35 s; self-tests about
2 s; total **115.6 s**, under 300.

Self-tests in prepare:
- **R1B:** each route vs FP64 at its m values, with probe rows and outliers; a route that fails is dropped by itself.
- **kcat:** at m = 1–128.
- **Tail, both modes, at T = 1/8/16/33/128/256** with local_start ≠ 0. Measured errors:
  - logits rel ≤ 9.4e-6;
  - xn rel ≤ 1.6e-4;
  - ids identical to FusedMuseSparkRouting;
  - weights max error ≤ 3e-7;
  - local ids identical to the r1 table.
- **K2A v3:** 8 cases through the layer entry point (remote ids −1), two calls each.
- **K1 v4:** 11 cases × 3 merge modes.
