## What changed vs k3_v10

215 top-level nodes are ast-identical. Changed: `_v8_experts`, `prepare`, `_v10_build_all`, `_v10_k2a31_build`.

- **K2A v3.3** (`kernels/muse_moe_w4a16/k2a_v33.py`, nonce k2a-v33-1) is the expert path in every bucket.
  - Sources are byte-identical; 15 host pieces are ast-identical after a K2A33_/_k2a33_ rename.
  - Its packers and BITPOS3 are asserted identical to v3.1's and reuse the layer's `_k2a31_` copies. Its pieces
    shared with v2.2 are asserted identical to k2a_v22's.
  - The layer call passes v3.3's per-bucket warm threshold (`V3_HOT_T`: 16 at bs128/256), as v3.3's own self-test
    and kernel_fn do.
- **Expert chain** in `_v8_experts`: v3.3 → v3.1 → v3 → v2.2 → Marlin. prepare builds an older kernel only for
  buckets every newer one failed: v3.1 adds ~35 s, v3 ~35 s, v2.2 ~45 s.
- **Combined CUDA build** now carries K1 + K2A v3.3 + R1B + tail, compiled for **sm_90a** (v3.3 uses wgmma).
  - v3.3 and v3.1 share entry points (`run3` / `attrs3v`, namespace `k3`), so v3.1 cannot be in the same module; it
    builds itself, and only as a fallback.
  - v3.3's variant cases and `@WGSPECS@` are substituted exactly as its own `build_ext_v3` does.
